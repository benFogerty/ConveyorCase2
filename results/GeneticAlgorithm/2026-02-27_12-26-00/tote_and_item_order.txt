TOTE LOADING ORDER AND ITEMS PER TOTE
============================================================
Tote contents (which items are in which tote) come from the data (orders_totes.csv).
This file shows the chosen ORDER of totes (first tote emptied completely, then next, etc.)
and the ORDER of items within each tote as loaded onto the conveyor.
Each line is one (order, shape, qty) from the data; qty = number of physical units.
So a tote can have more than 3 physical items (e.g. two lines with qty 3 and qty 2 = 5 units).
